Importance of Technology in Education

By Abhi | 29 August 2026

Technology has changed the way students learn and access information in modern education.

Online learning platforms make it easier for students to study from different locations.

Teachers can use digital tools to make lessons more interactive and interesting.

Students can find learning material quickly and communicate with teachers more easily.

However, technology should be used properly because too much screen time can also create problems.

Technology is most useful when it helps people learn and solve real problems.
